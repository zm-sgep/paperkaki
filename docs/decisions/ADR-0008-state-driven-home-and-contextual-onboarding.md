# ADR-0008: State-Driven Home and Contextual Onboarding

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Parents and children should not need to interpret a dense dashboard or learn the product through a tutorial.

## Decision
Home/Today is a state-driven decision surface with one dominant recommended action derived from current domain state.

There is no mandatory onboarding carousel. Guidance is contextual and appears only when needed.

The next-action policy is centralised and testable rather than scattered across UI components.

## Consequences
The product can still expose detailed reports, but they sit behind Progress or secondary disclosure. Home remains simple even as backend capabilities expand.
