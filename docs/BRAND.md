# PaperKaki Brand Baseline

## Product name

**PaperKaki**

## Positioning

PaperKaki helps parents prepare their children for their actual upcoming school assessments.

Core proposition:

> Upload the school's assessment scope. Generate a realistic mock paper. Mark it. Find the gaps. Practise what matters next.

## Name intent

"Kaki" is a familiar Singapore colloquial term for a companion or regular buddy.

The product name should therefore feel:
- local;
- friendly;
- approachable;
- practical;
- parent- and child-friendly.

## Tone

PaperKaki should feel like a helpful study companion, not a formal school administration system.

Use:
- simple language;
- short instructions;
- reassuring feedback;
- one obvious next action.

Avoid:
- bureaucratic education jargon;
- overly childish language;
- excessive gamification language;
- claims that the app can predict an actual school exam.

## Working tagline

**Your child's exam-prep kaki.**

Alternative:
**Prepare smarter for the paper that matters.**

Use the product name consistently as `PaperKaki` in user-facing copy.
